# CGTMSE Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF) Structure & Calculation

## 1. What is the Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF)?
The Annual Guarantee Fee (AGF) is the recurring fee paid by the Member Lending Institution (often passed on to the borrower) to maintain the credit guarantee cover active with CGTMSE. It is calculated annually on the outstanding balance (or sanctioned amount in the first year).

## 2. Standard Base Fee Slabs (per annum)

| Loan Facility Amount | Standard Annual Guarantee Fee Rate | Concessional Rate (Women, SC/ST, NER) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Up to ₹10 Lakh** | **0.37%** p.a. | **0.33%** p.a. (10% concession) |
| **Above ₹10 Lakh up to ₹50 Lakh** | **0.55%** p.a. | **0.495%** p.a. (10% concession) |
| **Above ₹50 Lakh up to ₹100 Lakh** | **0.60%** p.a. | **0.54%** p.a. (10% concession) |
| **Above ₹100 Lakh up to ₹200 Lakh** | **1.20%** p.a. | **1.08%** p.a. (10% concession) |
| **Above ₹200 Lakh up to ₹500 Lakh** | **1.35%** p.a. | **1.215%** p.a. (10% concession) |

## 3. Risk-Based Premium Surcharge
CGTMSE applies risk-based pricing adjustments depending on:
1. **MLI's Claim-to-Fee Ratio**: Lenders with higher historical default claim ratios attract a surcharge (up to 0.25% - 0.50%).
2. **Borrower NPA Track Record**: High-risk borrower profiles may carry an additional risk premium.
3. **Special Concessions**:
   - 10% concession on standard AGF for Women, SC/ST, Aspirational Districts, and ZED-certified enterprises.
   - Reduction in fees for units located in the North Eastern Region (NER).
