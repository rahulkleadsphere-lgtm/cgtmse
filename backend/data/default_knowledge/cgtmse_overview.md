# Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) - Overview & Framework

## 1. Establishment and Mandate
The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) was jointly set up by the **Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India** and the **Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI)**. It was launched on August 30, 2000, under the Credit Guarantee Scheme for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGS-I).

## 2. Core Objective
The primary objective of CGTMSE is to make collateral-free credit available to the Micro and Small Enterprise (MSE) sector. Under the scheme, credit guarantee cover is extended to Member Lending Institutions (MLIs) against credit facilities (term loans and/or working capital) extended by them without collateral security or third-party guarantee to eligible MSE borrowers.

## 3. Key Tenets
- **Collateral-Free Lending**: Borrowers are not required to provide any primary collateral security or third-party guarantee.
- **Credit Appraisal on Viability**: Banks and financial institutions appraise loan proposals based on project viability and commercial feasibility rather than asset-backing.
- **Mutual Risk Sharing**: CGTMSE absorbs up to 75% to 85% of the default risk, reassuring lenders and unlocking credit for first-generation entrepreneurs.
