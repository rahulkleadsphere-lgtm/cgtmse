# CGTMSE Eligibility Criteria & Covered Activities

## 1. Eligible Borrowers
- **Micro and Small Enterprises (MSEs)**: Both new and existing enterprises defined under the MSMED Act, 2006.
- **Manufacturing Enterprises**: Investment in plant and machinery up to ₹1 crore (Micro) and up to ₹10 crore (Small), with turnover limits up to ₹5 crore (Micro) and ₹50 crore (Small).
- **Service Enterprises**: Providing or rendering services within the MSMED investment and turnover limits.
- **Retail Trade**: Credit facilities extended to retail trade are eligible for guarantee cover up to **₹200 lakh (₹2 Crore)**.
- **Educational & Training Institutions**: Eligible for guarantee cover up to **₹200 lakh (₹2 Crore)**.

## 2. Eligible Member Lending Institutions (MLIs)
Loans must be disbursed through approved Member Lending Institutions registered with CGTMSE:
- Scheduled Commercial Banks (Public Sector Banks, Private Sector Banks, Foreign Banks)
- Regional Rural Banks (RRBs)
- Small Finance Banks (SFBs)
- Selected Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs)
- State Financial Corporations (SFCs)

## 3. Ineligible Activities & Borrowers
- **Medium Enterprises**: Medium enterprises (turnover > ₹50 crore or investment > ₹10 crore) are strictly NOT eligible.
- **Agriculture**: Direct agricultural operations, self-help groups (SHGs), and joint liability groups (JLGs) are not covered under CGS-I (separate NABARD/RBI credit guarantee frameworks apply).
- **Defaulting Units**: Borrowers having existing overdue accounts or non-performing assets (NPAs) across other institutions without restructuring approval.
- **Loans with Primary Collateral**: Facilities already secured by tangible collateral or third-party guarantee cannot be simultaneously covered under CGTMSE.
