# CGTMSE Required Documentation & Banker Appraisal Checklist

## 1. Statutory & Identity Documents
- **Udyam Registration Certificate (URC)**: Mandatory for all MSME applicants in India.
- **KYC Documents**: PAN Card and Aadhaar of Proprietor / Partners / Directors.
- **Proof of Business Address**: Electricity bill, rent agreement, or property tax receipt.
- **Constitutional Documents**: Partnership Deed, Memorandum & Articles of Association (MOA/AOA), Certificate of Incorporation (for Pvt Ltd/LLP).

## 2. Financial Documents
- **Past Financial Records**: Audited / CA-certified Balance Sheets and Profit & Loss statements for the last 2 to 3 years (for existing enterprises).
- **Income Tax Returns (ITR)**: Past 2 to 3 years of business and promoter ITRs with computation of income.
- **GST Returns**: GSTR-3B and GSTR-1 filings for the preceding 12 months.
- **Bank Account Statements**: Last 6 to 12 months operative current account bank statements.

## 3. Project & Credit Appraisal Checklist
- **Detailed Project Report (DPR)**: Comprehensive proposal detailing business model, manufacturing process, market feasibility, machinery quotation, civil works estimation, and working capital cycle.
- **Financial Projections**: Projected Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow Statement, and Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) for the proposed loan tenure.
- **Supplier Quotations**: Proforma invoices for capital goods, machinery, and equipment to be purchased.
- **Statutory Clearances**: Pollution Control Board (PCB) NOC, Fire clearance, Shop & Establishment license, or FSSAI registration (as applicable).

## 4. Crucial Reminders for Applicants
- **No Collateral Needed**: Do not offer land, buildings, or third-party guarantors for CGTMSE-eligible limits.
- **Direct Application**: Borrowers do not apply directly to CGTMSE. The loan application is submitted to the lending bank or NBFC, which subsequently logs into the CGTMSE portal to issue the guarantee cover.
