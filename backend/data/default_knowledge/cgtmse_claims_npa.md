# CGTMSE NPA Classification, Lock-in Period & Claim Settlement Procedures

## 1. Non-Performing Asset (NPA) Classification
An account is classified as NPA in accordance with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) prudential norms—typically when principal or interest remains overdue for more than 90 days.

## 2. Lock-in Period Rule
- The guarantee has a mandatory **lock-in period of 18 months** from either:
  1. The date of issue of the guarantee cover, OR
  2. The date of last disbursement of the credit facility, whichever is later.
- No claim can be lodged with CGTMSE prior to the expiration of this 18-month lock-in period.

## 3. Pre-requisites for Lodging a Claim
Before a Member Lending Institution (MLI) can invoke the guarantee and file a claim:
1. The borrower's account must have turned into an NPA.
2. The lock-in period of 18 months must have elapsed.
3. The MLI must have initiated legal recovery proceedings:
   - Notice under the SARFAESI Act, 2002; OR
   - Filing of an application before the Debt Recovery Tribunal (DRT); OR
   - Initiation of proceedings in a Civil Court or Lok Adalat; OR
   - Action under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act for dishonored cheques.

## 4. Claim Settlement Stages
- **First Installment (75%)**: CGTMSE pays **75% of the eligible guaranteed amount** upon verification of NPA, legal notice proof, and claim documentation.
- **Second / Final Installment (25%)**: The remaining **25%** is settled once the legal recovery proceedings have reached final decree, execution, or recovery conclusion. Any recovered amounts from the borrower must be shared pro-rata with CGTMSE.
